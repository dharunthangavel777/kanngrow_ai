If Kangrow is focused on **AI-powered E-commerce Business Building**, then your backend should not be built around screens (Chat, Dashboard, etc.).

It should be built around **Business Capabilities**.

# Most Important Part (The Brain)

```text
AI ROUTER
│
├── Product Discovery
├── Product Validation
├── Market Analysis
├── Competitor Analysis
├── Business Planning
├── Growth Coaching
└── General Chat
```

Example:

User:

```text
I have ₹50,000. What ecommerce business should I start?
```

Router detects:

```text
Intent:
Product Discovery
```

Automatically runs:

```text
Idea Engine
+
Market Intelligence
+
Validation Engine
+
Recommendation Engine
```

Then combines everything into one answer.
